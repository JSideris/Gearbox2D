BVH Mask Biasing Performance Study Results
==========================================
Date: 2026-01-05
Scenarios: 1000 objects, 10 categories (self-collide only)

Scenario 1: High Density (All objects overlapping at 0,0)
---------------------------------------------------------
Weight 0 (SAH only):    1.12 ms (Baseline)
Weight 5 (Aggressive):  0.09 ms (12.4x speedup)
Weight 1 (Balanced):    0.09 ms (12.4x speedup)

Scenario 2: Low Density (Objects spread randomly 0-100)
-------------------------------------------------------
Weight 0 (SAH only):    0.06 ms (Baseline)
Weight 5 (Aggressive):  0.10 ms (1.6x slowdown)
Weight 1 (Balanced):    0.05 ms (1.2x speedup)

Analysis
--------
- Aggressive biasing (Weight 5) is fantastic for high-density "collision layers" but hurts 
  spatially distinct objects by creating loose bounding volumes.
- Balanced biasing (Weight 1) captures almost all the benefits of layer-clustering 
  without the spatial penalties. It actually outperformed the baseline even in 
  the spread scenario by reducing "noisy" branch merges between incompatible layers.

Final Recommendation: Use maskWeight = 1.0f.
